    (09) ANSWERS

    FAQ.

    What is the difference between monthly and per project?

    A project has an end date. We make the film, hand over every file, and it is yours. Monthly is for brands that need video every week: one active request at a time, and you can pause any month you do not need us.

    Is that the final price?

    It is a real starting number, not a teaser. Scope is the only thing that moves it: an extra cut, a second concept, a deadline that needs more hands. We lock the figure on the first call and it holds from there.

    How long will it take?

    Five to seven days for a single advert, two to three weeks for a brand film. We only take a few clients a month, so your start date is real and not a place in a queue.

    Is everything made with AI?

    No. Ideas, scripts, edits and sound are made by people. AI generates the footage we could not otherwise afford to shoot, and we mix in live footage when a real product or person has to be on screen.

    Who owns the finished video?

    You do. Every project ships with full commercial rights and the source files, with nothing to renew.

    What do you need from me to start?

    A short brief, your brand assets, and one person who can make decisions. The clearer the goal, the faster we move.

    MORE QUESTIONS?

    Reach out anytime. We are happy to answer before you commit to working together.
