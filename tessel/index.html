Is the free plan really free?

Yes. You get 500 runs a month and three workflows for as long as you like, with no card on file. You only pay when you need more runs or more people.

Can anything be sent without approval?

Only if you decide it can. Every workflow starts with approval required on anything a customer will see. You can relax that step by step once you trust what it writes.

What happens when the AI is unsure?

It has to quote its source for every answer. If the quote is not in the original message, Tessel marks that answer unknown and the reply asks for it instead of guessing.

Which tools does it connect to?

The common CRMs, help desks, inboxes, calendars and ledgers, plus anything with a webhook or an API. If you already run n8n, Tessel can call your existing workflows as steps.

Where is our data stored?

In the region you choose when you sign up. Your data is never used to train models, and Enterprise plans can bring their own model keys.

Can we change plans later?

At any time. Upgrades apply straight away and downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.
